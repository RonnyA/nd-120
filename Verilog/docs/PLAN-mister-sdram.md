## What is already true (surveyed 01-SEP-2026, file:line in the survey)

- The Tang's stack is the model, not the Nexys DDR2 cache: the DE10-Nano
  SDRAM module is plain SDR SDRAM on fabric pins, exactly the Tang's
  situation. `fpga/tang-nano-20k/sdram-bridge/sdram18.v` (controller,
  adapted from nand2mario, Apache-2.0) is plain Verilog-2001 with no vendor
  primitive; `MEM_RAM_49_SDRAM.v` (the sheet-49 bridge) likewise. The only
  Gowin pieces are the rPLL and the `O_sdram_*` port names.
- The bridge meets the CPU's HARD read deadline directly: row at OSC N,
  CAS at N+2, data must be on DD at N+4, and the bridge delivers at fast
  edge 2N+8 = N+4 with `clk2x = 2 x OSC`, edge-aligned, from the same PLL,
  plus a 180-degree copy for the chip. Refresh is generated by the bridge
  in its idle slots (the board's own refresh chain is inactive). Banks map
  BANK0, BANK2 in physical order; BANK1 is absent.
- MiSTer today: all SDRAM pins tied to Z in `nd120.sv:32`; the 40 pin
  locations and IO settings (fast in/out registers) are already correct in
  `nd120.qsf:109-153`; `nd120.sdc` has NO SDRAM constraint; `clk_cpu` is
  20 MHz from `rtl/pll_cpu.v`, `clk_sys` 40 MHz from `rtl/pll.v`.
- The `MEM_43.v` backend `ifdef` chain already threads `MAIN_RAM_SDRAM`'s
  ports up through `ND3202D.v` and `ND120_CORE.v`; only the pin widths
  (32-bit DQ / 11-bit A / 4 DQM on the Tang vs 16 / 13 / 2 here) differ.
- Proof that exists: `fpga/tang-nano-20k/sdram-bridge/sim` runs one bench
  four ways (`test`, `test-pack16`, `test-pack16-part`,
  `test-storage-port`) against a behavioural `sdram_model.v`, sampling
  late in N+4 and N+5 for the deadline; standalone memtests in
  `sdram-test/` and `sdram18-test/`. Missing: a `test-memchain-sdram`
  behind the real MEM_RAMC_50 PALs (the DDR2 one exists to clone).
- Chip geometry: the emu port has A[12:0], BA[1:0], DQ[15:0]. Which SDRAM
  module is fitted (32 / 64 / 128 MB) decides rows x columns and the
  refresh interval (4096 rows: 15 us; 8192 rows: 7.8 us). NOT KNOWN from
  the repo - Ronny to say.

## Decisions taken

- Reuse the Tang controller and bridge; no cache front end (the cache in
  the DDR2 backend exists because MIG latency is variable; SDR SDRAM is
  deterministic).
- New backend define `MAIN_RAM_SDRAM16` with its own modules
  (`sdram16.v`, `MEM_RAM_49_SDRAM16.v`) rather than widening the Tang's
  ports: the Tang build stays byte-identical.
- 16-bit bus means one ND word per SDRAM location: no PACK16 lane masks,
  no half-word address bit. Parity is still computed on the read path,
  never stored (`docs/nd120-parity-analysis.md`).
- `clk2x` = 40 MHz and its 180-degree copy come from the CPU PLL, so the
  fixed 2:1 phase relation the bridge relies on holds.

## Phase 1 - the 16-bit controller, sim only

- [ ] `Verilog/fpga/mister/rtl/sdram16.v` from `sdram18.v`: DATA_WIDTH 16,
      DQM[1:0], A[12:0], ROW/COL/BANK widths as real parameters that slice
      `addr` (the Tang file hardcodes `addr[21:20]`/`addr[19:9]`), CAS and
      tRP/tRCD/tRC/tWR/tMRD retuned for 40 MHz from the module datasheet,
      init counter wide enough for 200 us at 40 MHz. Keep the read-DQM
      latency-2 lesson.
- [ ] `sdram_model16.v` (behavioural) from the Tang's `sdram_model.v` with
      the same geometry; `sdram16_test_tb.v` memtest (write+verify a
      region, refresh cadence check). `make test-sdram16` in
      `fpga/mister/sim`, registered.

## Phase 2 - the bridge, sim only

- [ ] `Verilog/fpga/mister/rtl/MEM_RAM_49_SDRAM16.v` from
      `MEM_RAM_49_SDRAM.v`: keep the FSM, the OR-accumulated write capture,
      the refresh scheduling, the computed-parity read path and the
      BANK0/BANK2 map verbatim; drop pack16 and `acc32`; address
      `{bank_q, row_q, AA_9_0}` = 2M words = 4 MB in the bottom of the
      chip; `CPU_PART_ROWS` kept; storage port kept behind
      `ND_STORAGE_PORT` but NOT used on the MiSTer (images come from the
      HPS).
- [ ] Retarget the four-way bridge bench (`mem_ram_49_sdram_tb.v` +
      model) to the 16-bit pair: `test`, `test-part`, plus the deadline
      sampling. Registered.
- [ ] `test-memchain-sdram16` behind the real PALs, cloned from
      `MEM_CHAIN_DDR2_tb.v`.
